# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Recruiters, engineering leaders, research collaborators, and technically curious peers who want to understand Ruchir Kalokhe's capabilities, judgment, and body of work quickly.

## Product Purpose

Present Ruchir as a backend engineer moving deeper into AI research. One calm page: about, a deep work index, and short notes. Make strongest projects easy to inspect, communicate technical depth without posturing, and create a clear path to start a conversation.

## Brand Personality

Precise, restless, and grounded. European calm with imperial restraint: Instrument Serif for presence, Space Mono for systems clarity. Soft chalk field, charcoal ink, oxblood accent.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic dark SaaS pages, editorial-serif portfolio templates, fake terminal interfaces, coordinate and weather decoration, numbered section scaffolds, neon AI gradients, and motion added only for spectacle.

## Design Principles

1. Show the work before describing the ambition.
2. Let technical depth feel calm and legible.
3. Use motion to explain hierarchy and depth (Lenis scroll, GSAP perspective, project-reactive paper dither).
4. Keep every interaction immediate and interruptible.
5. Make the site distinctive without hiding familiar navigation.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Maintain keyboard-visible focus, readable contrast, stable mobile layouts, and complete reduced-motion and reduced-transparency fallbacks. WebGL is decorative and must never carry essential content.
